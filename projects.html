Stealth Guard AI

Unreal EngineC++

A reusable, Assassin's Creed-style guard AI built in C++ for Unreal Engine as my subsystem in a group tech demo. I redesigned a lab finite state machine into five states (Patrol, Suspicious, Alerted, Search and Death) driven by a detection meter that fills the longer and closer the player stays in a guard's view. Alerted guards stop and shoot at range, with separate stop and resume-chase distances to avoid stuttering, and track fast enough that circling them doesn't work. Detection lives in its own component, every value is tunable in the editor, and guards can be given patrol routes at runtime so they plug into my partner's procedurally generated levels.
